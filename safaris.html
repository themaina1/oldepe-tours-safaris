Discover Kenya's Safari Destinations

From the legendary plains of the Maasai Mara to the elephant country of Amboseli, discover Kenya's remarkable wildlife, landscapes and unforgettable safari experiences.

Where the wild awaits

Explore Kenya's finest wilderness

Kenya is home to some of Africa's most remarkable national parks and wildlife conservancies. Each destination has its own character, wildlife and scenery. Explore these special places with Oldepe Tours & Safaris and let us help you plan your next adventure.

01

Maasai Mara National Reserve

Big Five Great Migration Hot-air ballooning

The Maasai Mara is one of Kenya's most celebrated safari destinations. Its sweeping grasslands and riverine forests support an extraordinary variety of wildlife, including lions, elephants, cheetahs and leopards. Between July and October, the reserve is part of the spectacular Great Migration.

02

Amboseli National Park

Elephant herds Mount Kilimanjaro Scenic views

Famous for its large elephant herds and beautiful views of Mount Kilimanjaro, Amboseli offers memorable wildlife encounters. Its open plains and wetlands attract elephants, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and a rich variety of birdlife.

Browse Amboseli safaris →
Iconic landscapes
03

Lake Nakuru National Park

Flamingos Rhino sanctuary Rift Valley

Set in Kenya's beautiful Great Rift Valley, Lake Nakuru is known for its birdlife, scenic lake views and important rhino sanctuary. Visitors can look out for both black and white rhinos, giraffes, buffaloes and other wildlife.

Rift Valley wildlife
04

Ol Pejeta Conservancy

Black rhinos Conservation Big Five

Located in Laikipia, Ol Pejeta is a leading wildlife conservation destination. It is especially known for its rhino conservation work, diverse wildlife and beautiful views towards Mount Kenya.

Mount Kenya Mountain Region

Mountain scenery Forest walks Adventure

Discover the dramatic scenery around Africa's second-highest mountain. The Mount Kenya region offers forest landscapes, fresh mountain air, unique high-altitude environments and wonderful opportunities for nature lovers.

Aberdare National Park

Waterfalls Mountain forests Wildlife

The Aberdares offer a refreshing escape into Kenya's highland wilderness. Explore beautiful forests, waterfalls, moorlands and rich wildlife in one of the country's most scenic mountain environments.

07

Tsavo National Parks

Red elephants Vast wilderness Big Five

Discover the vast wilderness of Tsavo East and Tsavo West. Famous for its red elephants, dramatic landscapes and diverse wildlife, Tsavo offers an authentic safari experience across some of Kenya's largest protected areas.
