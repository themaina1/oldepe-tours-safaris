Welcome to Oldepe Tours & Safaris

Oldepe Tours & Safaris is a Kenya-based travel company focused on helping visitors discover the beauty, wildlife and culture of Kenya.

From exciting wildlife safaris to private tours and travel experiences, our aim is to make your journey comfortable, enjoyable and memorable.

We understand that every traveller is different. That is why we welcome enquiries for both planned safari packages and customised journeys.
