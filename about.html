About Oldepe Tours & Safaris

Discover Kenya through authentic wildlife, safari, cultural and nature experiences designed around your travel interests.

Discover Kenya With Us

Oldepe Tours & Safaris is focused on creating memorable Kenya travel experiences for visitors who want to explore the country's wildlife, landscapes, culture and natural heritage.

From the open plains of the Maasai Mara and the elephants of Amboseli to the flamingos of Lake Nakuru, the rhinos of Ol Pejeta and the dramatic landscapes of Mount Kenya and Tsavo, Kenya offers an incredible variety of experiences.

We help travellers plan private safaris, wildlife experiences, cultural experiences, airport transfers and custom journeys according to their interests, available time and preferred destinations.

Destinations We Explore

Discover some of Kenya's most remarkable wildlife and natural destinations.

Maasai Mara

Famous for abundant wildlife, big cats and the Great Migration.

Amboseli National Park

Known for its elephants and spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Lake Nakuru National Park

A Rift Valley wildlife destination known for birdlife, flamingos and rhinos.

Ol Pejeta Conservancy

A leading conservation destination with exceptional rhino and wildlife experiences.

Mount Kenya

A dramatic mountain destination offering beautiful highland landscapes and nature experiences.

Aberdare National Park

A beautiful highland ecosystem with forests, mountains and diverse wildlife.

Tsavo National Parks

Vast wilderness areas famous for elephants, wildlife and dramatic landscapes.

Samburu

A unique northern Kenya safari destination with distinctive wildlife and landscapes.
