About Oldepe Tours & Safaris

Discover Kenya with a team committed to creating enjoyable, memorable and personal travel experiences.

Discover Kenya With Us

Kenya is a country of extraordinary wildlife, landscapes, cultures and people. At Oldepe Tours & Safaris, our aim is to help visitors experience the best of Kenya in a way that is comfortable, enjoyable and memorable.

From the streets of Nairobi to the open savannahs of Kenya's national parks, we offer a range of tours and safari experiences for travellers looking to discover the country.

Whether you are visiting Kenya for the first time, returning for another safari, travelling with family or looking for a private experience, we can help you plan a journey around your interests and schedule.
